# Market study: otc.fam

A link for one OTC token deal, like a calendar link: open it, check it, send the tokens there.

**No market research has been done yet.** This page holds only what the creator has said about the
product, in his words, and the questions that are still open. It has no market numbers and names no
competitor, because nobody has looked them up. It will be rewritten once the blanks below are filled.

## What the creator said

- People still do OTC deals over Telegram, and it gets complicated.
- otc.fam is a link for one deal, like a calendar link: "otc.fam/…", then a code.
- It is totally non-custodial.
- The page looks like Uniswap, not like a SaaS form.
- The use: "I sent you my otc.fam link, can you check it and send the tokens there?"
